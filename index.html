How to Play

Continuous Pitch Control: Moving your mouse or touch across the Horizontal (X) Axis continuously changes the audio frequency.

Volume Control: Moving vertically on the Vertical (Y) Axis controls the volume amplitude (up = louder, down = quieter).

Pitch Visualizers:

  • Continuous Strip: Linear view with piano semitone reference markers.
  • Radial Ring: Polar coordinate view displaying pitch class rotation.
